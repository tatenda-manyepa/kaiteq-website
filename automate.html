A few useful answers

Before we begin.

Does every automation use AI?

No. A straightforward rules-based workflow may be the better choice. AI is considered only when it serves the task.

Will my team stay in control?

We agree approval steps, access permissions and exceptions with you before configuring the workflow.

Are email, forms and AI separate extra workflows?

No. These are possible components of the one to three workflows agreed in your proposal, not additional automations on top. Custom connectors, unsupported systems and complex migrations require separate scoping.

How do you test it?

We test the agreed workflow with representative inputs and exceptions, then review the result with you before handover.
