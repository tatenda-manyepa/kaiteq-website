What we agree together

Clarity before code.

  • The users, problem and intended outcome
  • Features, integrations and what is outside scope
  • Budget, milestones and acceptance criteria
  • Data access, security and operational requirements
  • Handover, ownership arrangements and support options

Pricing depends on the agreed requirements. Third-party services and recurring costs are identified in the proposal.

Common questions

Room for your questions.

Can we start small?

Yes. We can scope an initial version around the most useful features and identify what could follow later.

Can you improve something we already use?

We can review the existing system, access and constraints, then advise whether extending, integrating or replacing it is practical.

Who owns the finished product?

Ownership, source access, licences and third-party dependencies are agreed in the project terms before work begins.

What happens after launch?

We agree handover and support arrangements as part of the proposal. Continuing maintenance and monitoring can be scoped separately.
