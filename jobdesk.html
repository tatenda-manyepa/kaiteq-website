A few useful answers

Before we begin.

Who is JobDesk for?

Independent service businesses and solo entrepreneurs, including people running a business alongside another job. We review whether your booking journey fits before onboarding.

Can I keep my existing website?

Yes. JobDesk can be purchased independently. Any connection to an existing website is reviewed at setup; specialist integration work is quoted separately.

What is included in £49/month?

Access to the agreed solo-business JobDesk plan, the WhatsApp assistant and normal product support. We confirm user, calendar, messaging and AI allowances before you subscribe.

Can I add tailored workflows?

Yes. Work beyond the standard booking journey is scoped and quoted separately under Custom Solutions.
