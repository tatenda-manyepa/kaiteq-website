A few useful answers

Before we begin.

Is this a financial adviser or business manager?

Growth provides AI-assisted information and planning support. It is not a human business manager, accountant or regulated financial adviser. You remain responsible for decisions; specialist financial, tax or investment advice should come from an appropriately qualified professional.

What information does it use?

Your goals and the business data agreed at setup. Sources, permissions and included connections are confirmed in the proposal. Missing or incomplete information limits the insights; you can review and correct inputs.

Does Growth include social media posting?

Not in the standard package. Content drafting, approval and scheduling workflows can be quoted as an optional addition.

Will it guarantee growth?

No. It helps you identify options and measure progress. Results depend on the quality of the data, the actions you choose and your business circumstances.
