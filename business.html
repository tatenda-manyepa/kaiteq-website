A few useful answers

Before we begin.

Do I need to buy a website to use JobDesk?

No. JobDesk is also available independently for £149 setup plus £49/month. Choose that route if you already have a website or mainly work through WhatsApp.

Will it answer every customer question?

The assistant works within the services, answers and booking rules agreed with you. Unusual requests and questions outside that scope are passed back to you.

Can tradespeople use it?

Yes, where the agreed booking journey fits. A plumber can arrange a visit or assessment without promising a fixed-price repair. Service areas, travel gaps and urgent requests need agreed rules.

Is bespoke development included?

Business includes configuration of the standard JobDesk product. Specialist integrations, new product features and additional workflows are quoted under Custom Solutions.
